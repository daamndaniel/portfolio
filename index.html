How I work

Serious about the work. Easy to work with.

I care deeply about good design, but I don't believe great work comes from one person having all the answers. I ask questions early, share ideas openly, welcome feedback, and enjoy the messy middle of figuring things out together.

Partnering with product, engineering, stakeholders, or fellow designers, I aim to bring clarity, good energy, and momentum to the room.

Good work is serious. The process doesn't have to be.

A thoughtful teammate with a growth mindset.

Working with me
01

I bring an opinion, not an ego.

I care about craft and I'm comfortable advocating for a strong point of view. But the best solution rarely belongs to one person, so I listen closely, invite feedback, and let the strongest idea win.

02

I stay calm when things get messy.

Ambiguity is part of the process. I enjoy breaking complex problems into clear next steps, helping teams move forward without losing sight of the people we're designing for.

03

I'm always learning.

New tools, unfamiliar industries, feedback, and tough problems are all opportunities to grow. I'm naturally curious and excited by teammates who teach me something new.
