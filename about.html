01 · How I got here

From multimedia to product

I studied Multimedia and Audiovisual Production at Universidad de las Américas in Quito. That is a long way of saying I learned to tell stories with images, sound, and timing before I ever learned to design an interface.

That background still shapes how I work. I think about pacing, about what someone notices first, and about the moment a screen either earns trust or quietly loses it. Six years in, the job is still what it was back then: removing noise until the point becomes obvious.

I like research that changes my mind and critique that gets specific; mostly I like building alongside people who care about the small details as much as the big decisions.

05 · Off the clock

Loud rooms, slow evenings

Music is the constant, whether that means a concert, a festival, or the same three albums streaming on repeat while I work. I love digital media; I just also love owning things. A few years ago my girlfriend and I started buying vinyl, and between eight records, half a dozen CDs, and a growing stack of Nintendo Switch cartridges, collecting has quietly become its own hobby.

Streaming is where I listen. The shelf is where I keep the things I love most.
